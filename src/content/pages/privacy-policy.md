#### Introduction

Astroplate ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at [https://astroplate.jadoo.bond](https://astroplate.jadoo.bond). By using this website, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

#### Information We Collect

We collect a small amount of information, in two ways:

- **Information you provide directly.** If you contact us through the [contact page](/contact), you may share your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We use this only to read and reply to your message.
- **Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server log data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, and the time of your visit. This data is used for security, troubleshooting, and understanding how the site is used. It is not used to identify you personally.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to anyone.

#### Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. This website may use cookies to remember basic preferences and to understand how visitors use the site. You can refuse cookies through your browser settings; the site will still work, though some features may be less convenient. For more detail, see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy).

#### Advertising and Google AdSense

This website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your browsing.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
