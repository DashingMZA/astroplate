#### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store on your device. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as your preferences, and help site owners understand how visitors use their site. Cookies cannot run programs or deliver viruses, and they do not give a website access to other files on your device.

#### How This Website Uses Cookies

Astroplate, at [https://astroplate.jadoo.bond](https://astroplate.jadoo.bond), may use the following types of cookies:

- **Strictly necessary cookies.** These are required for the website to function — for example, remembering basic settings and keeping the site secure. The site will not work properly without them.
- **Preference cookies.** These remember choices you make, such as your preferred colour scheme (light or dark mode), so you do not have to set them again on your next visit.
- **Analytics cookies.** If analytics tools are enabled, they collect anonymous information about how visitors use the site — such as which pages are visited most often — so we can improve the content. This data does not identify you personally.
- **Advertising cookies.** If advertisements are displayed on the site, advertising partners such as Google may set cookies to show you ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. These cookies are set by the advertising partner, not by us. For more detail, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

#### Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on this site may be set by third parties, including advertising and analytics providers. We do not control these cookies. You can review Google's use of cookies at [policies.google.com/technologies/cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

#### Managing and Deleting Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings: most browsers let you block some or all cookies, or delete cookies already stored. Please note that blocking cookies may affect how the website works for you. You can also opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) and at [www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
